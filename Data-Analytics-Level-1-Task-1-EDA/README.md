Task 1 — EDA on Retail Sales Data

Objective

Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on retail sales data to understand sales trends, customer behaviour, product performance, and business opportunities.

Tools & Technologies

Python

Pandas

Matplotlib

Seaborn

Jupyter Notebook

Analysis Performed

Dataset shape, columns, data types, and null-value analysis

Descriptive statistics of numerical variables

Monthly sales trend analysis

Quarterly sales trend analysis

Customer age-group distribution

Gender-wise customer analysis

Top 10 best-selling products/brands

Revenue analysis by product category

Correlation heatmap of numerical variables

Additional visualization for deeper business insight

Actionable business recommendations

Dataset

The analysis uses the retail_sales_fmcg_2024.csv dataset.

Files

Task_1_EDA_Retail_Sales.ipynb — Jupyter Notebook containing the complete EDA

retail_sales_fmcg_2024.csv — Retail sales dataset

Key Outcome

The analysis identifies sales trends, customer patterns, category performance, and other insights that can support inventory planning, customer targeting, and business decision-making.

Conclusion

The findings from this EDA can help businesses improve inventory management, identify high-performing categories, understand customer behaviour, and make data-driven decisions.
